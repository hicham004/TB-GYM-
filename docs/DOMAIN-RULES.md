# TB Gym Domain Rules

Source: complete review of `plan.docx` and the legacy application, 2026-08-15.

This document turns the product description into rules that can be enforced and tested. A
rule marked **proposed** is the safest interpretation of an ambiguous request and needs
product confirmation before its feature is implemented. Open decisions are collected at the
end.

## 1. System-wide rules

**SYS-001** The backend and database are authoritative. The SPA cannot grant access, accept a
contradictory date, calculate a persisted target, or bypass a state transition by changing a
request payload.

**SYS-002** Every coach-owned record belongs to exactly one tenant. Every read and write must
be authorized against that tenant. Platform administrators do not silently become tenant
coaches; support access must be explicit and audited.

**SYS-003** Important changes record UTC time, actor, tenant, previous version, and reason
where appropriate. Payment, access block, subscription, program prescription, 1RM, and
sensitive intake changes require durable history.

**SYS-004** Historical paid/completed records are cancelled, superseded, or archived, not
hard deleted. This conflicts with the plan's suggestion to delete a previous program to make
room for another; replacement must preserve history.

**SYS-005** Commands that can be retried, including invitations, payment registration,
workout completion, imports, notifications, and provider callbacks, must be idempotent.

**SYS-006** Mutable aggregates use optimistic concurrency. A stale coach screen must receive
a conflict instead of overwriting a newer payment, program, note, or profile update.

**SYS-007** Units are explicit. Bodyweight and training load are stored in a canonical unit
(proposed: kilograms) with the entered value/unit retained when conversion matters. Food
amounts carry a unit and preparation basis. Money carries amount and ISO currency.

**SYS-008** Instants are UTC. Calendar dates are interpreted in the tenant's IANA time zone.
A tenant must have one configured week start (proposed: ISO Monday). Do not use the API
server's local clock as a business calendar.

## 2. Identity, tenants, clients, and invitations

**CLI-001** A person has one global login identity and may have separate tenant memberships
and client profiles under multiple coaches. An email match links only through a verified
invitation; it must never expose one coach's profile to another coach.

**CLI-002** A tenant is a coach workspace. Owner and Coach can manage clients. Client can
access only their own permitted tenant data. Platform Admin is a separate global role.

**CLI-003** An invitation may be email, WhatsApp, or both, is single use, expires, and records
its sender, recipient, tenant, status, and acceptance time. Resend/accept commands are
idempotent and a revoked invitation cannot be accepted.

**CLI-004** The coach may prefill onboarding data or allow the client to complete it. A
prefilled field is displayed rather than requested again. During the onboarding draft, the
client may correct fields they are explicitly allowed to supply. The approved field
ownership and audit policy is recorded in `docs/adr/0004-client-field-ownership.md`.

**CLI-005** Account creation time is generated by the server. Age is never stored as a
long-lived value; birth date is stored and age is derived for a specified date.

**CLI-006** Intake supports name, email, phone, birth date, sex used by the selected formula,
height, initial bodyweight, self photo, allergies, medications, previous injuries, work type,
average steps, sport/training background, food preferences/aversions, goals, and notes.
Required versus optional fields and allowed sex/formula choices remain a product decision.

**CLI-007** Initial bodyweight creates a dated progress observation. "Current bodyweight" is
derived from the latest valid observation, not maintained as a second editable number on the
profile.

**CLI-008** Allergies, medications, injury history, photos, and body measurements are
sensitive personal or health-adjacent data. Access is least privilege, exports and deletion
are auditable, retention is defined, and logs/analytics must not contain raw values.

**CLI-009** Coach block and platform block are separate facts with actor, reason, effective
time, and optional reversal. The client cannot change either fact.

## 3. Subscriptions, payments, programs, and access

**SUB-001** A subscription is a commercial service period for one tenant/client. Periods use
`[startDate, endDateExclusive)`, so a four-week period starting July 27 ends exclusively on
August 24 and its last active date is August 23.

**SUB-002** A client cannot have overlapping live subscription periods in the same tenant.
Drafts may overlap while being edited, but activation must be serialized and protected by a
PostgreSQL exclusion constraint. Cancelled/rejected drafts do not block a new period.

**SUB-003** A client cannot have overlapping active assigned training mesocycles. **Proposed:**
each assigned training program belongs to exactly one subscription and normally shares its
service period. If diet and training can have different periods, that must be modeled
explicitly rather than duplicating unsynchronized dates.

**SUB-004** Subscription duration has one source of truth: start plus end-exclusive. Week
count is validated/derived, not a third independently editable fact. Training week dates,
unlock dates, renewal time, diet applicability, and progress grouping derive from that
period.

**SUB-005** Payment registration is append-only. A new payment never overwrites an earlier
amount. Each entry records amount, currency, method/reference, received time, registering
coach, and reversal/refund if any. The subscription exposes a derived payment standing.

**SUB-006** The initial manual-payment policy in `plan.docx` is:

1. No active membership, platform block, or coach block: access denied.
2. Valid membership but no current paid subscription: profile-only access with payment CTA.
3. Current subscription with pending/overdue payment: profile-only access.
4. Current subscription in acceptable payment standing: full assigned-content access.

The ordering above is authoritative and must be evaluated server-side for every protected
resource. Whether `Waived` grants full access is proposed as yes.

**SUB-007** "Partially paid means proportionally paid weeks" is not safe without a precise
contract. Amount paid does not automatically identify which dates are covered, especially
with discounts, installments, currencies, or refunds. Until defined, payment is either
accepted for the subscription or outstanding; installment support requires an explicit
schedule.

**SUB-008** A renewal reminder is scheduled for three tenant-calendar days before the end
date, sent at most once per channel/version, and cancelled or recalculated if the period is
changed, cancelled, renewed, or blocked.

**SUB-009** Blocking takes effect immediately for authorization even if a session is active.
Unblocking does not create a subscription or mark a payment paid.

**SUB-010** Account, subscription, program, and payment statuses are state machines. Arbitrary
boolean combinations are not accepted from clients. All transitions are authorized,
validated, concurrent-write protected, and audited.

## 4. Program templates and assigned snapshots

**TRN-001** A training template is coach-owned reusable content. Assigning it creates a deep,
client-specific snapshot with a reference to template/version provenance. Later template
edits do not mutate an assigned mesocycle.

**TRN-002** Assigned snapshots receive a per-client sequence/name such as `Meso 1`; the coach
may rename the display label. Sequence identity remains stable and unique within the client.

**TRN-003** The assigned snapshot belongs to one client and subscription. Copying must include
weeks, days, prescriptions, set overrides, notes intended as template content, visibility
rules, exercise version references, and strength calculation inputs. Completion data and
client notes are never copied from a previous assignment.

**TRN-004** The coach can add/remove/reorder weeks and days while an assignment is editable.
Changes to started/completed work require an audit trail and must not silently remap existing
logs to a different date.

**TRN-005** A program supports a week-by-day grid. Each day contains ordered exercises. Each
exercise prescription supports sets, reps, load, target RPE or RIR, and optional per-set
overrides. A shared value is the default; only differing sets need overrides.

**TRN-006** Client-visible future weeks are calculated from program start, tenant time zone,
and week index. The coach may override visibility for one week or reveal the entire program.
The API filters locked content; hiding it only in Angular is not protection.

**TRN-007** The phrase "international calendar" is not precise. **Proposed:** weeks start on
the tenant's configured ISO Monday and the first program week starts on the subscription
start date even when that date is not Monday. Product confirmation is required.

**TRN-008** Workout completion is one idempotent event for the assigned day/client, with time
and actor. A completion can be corrected under a defined policy and audit trail. Gamification
consumes the event; it does not own workout truth.

**TRN-009** Coach notes and client notes have separate author and visibility policies. Notes
may target an assignment, week, training day, prescribed exercise, diet plan, meal slot, or
meal choice using explicit typed references. A client cannot edit a coach-only note.

**TRN-010** The note "main lifts non modifiable, accessories client can modify" conflicts
with coach-only program adjustment. **Proposed:** the coach marks each prescription as
locked, client-adjustable, or client-suggestion-only; all client changes retain the original
prescription and are audited.

## 5. Strength, 1RM, RPE, RIR, and progression

**STR-001** A 1RM observation belongs to tenant, client, exercise, value/unit, effective time,
source (tested or estimated), formula/model version, and optional evidence/notes. Updating a
client's current 1RM creates a new version rather than rewriting history.

**STR-002** Assigning 1RM values to a program snapshots the exact strength profile/version
used by its calculations. Later strength updates do not silently change prescribed loads;
the coach explicitly recalculates/accepts a new snapshot.

**STR-003** RPE and RIR are related but not two independently exact fields. In common
resistance-training usage, RPE 10 roughly means 0 RIR, RPE 9 roughly 1 RIR, and so on, but
the relationship is subjective and less meaningful far from failure. Store one canonical
target plus the conversion model/version; derive the companion display where appropriate.

**STR-004** The plan requests both RPE and RIR from 0 to 10. That is not a sound universal
validation rule. RPE prescriptions commonly use a bounded exertion scale, while useful RIR
is normally a non-negative estimate and does not need to mirror the full scale. Final ranges
and allowed increments (such as 0.5 RPE) need coach confirmation.

**STR-005** There is no universal "most sophisticated" load formula. A progression model must
be named and versioned, publish its source/table, state valid rep/RPE ranges, round to the
available plate increment, and return an explanation. Out-of-range inputs require coach
review rather than fabricated precision.

**STR-006** Duplicating week 1 into later weeks creates new prescriptions and applies a
deterministic progression rule to each eligible exercise. Re-running with the same source
and rule is idempotent or requires an explicit replace confirmation. Manual overrides are
identified and not silently overwritten.

**STR-007** Calculation from a program 1RM and calculation from week-1 working load are
different models. The coach chooses one source per progression operation; the system records
it. It never mixes them implicitly.

## 6. Nutrition, recipes, and calorie targets

**NUT-001** Ingredient records include food identity, quantity, unit, raw/cooked/preparation
basis, provider/source, source record/version, protein, carbohydrate, fat, calories, and any
manual override with actor/reason. Raw and cooked weights are not interchangeable.

**NUT-002** Recipe totals derive from ingredient lines under one calculation policy. The
recipe records yield/servings and preparation instructions. A manual total cannot silently
diverge from its ingredients; an override is explicit, versioned, and attributable.

**NUT-003** Direct recipe import uses a licensed/authorized nutrition or recipe provider,
structured data, or user-supplied URL where terms permit. Generic Google scraping is not a
reliable or legally safe data source. Imported data keeps provenance and requires review,
especially preparation basis and serving yield.

**NUT-004** AI-generated meals are drafts, never trusted nutrition facts. Output must match a
schema, identify uncertain ingredients/units, pass domain validation, and be approved by the
coach before entering the library. Provider failure produces a visible error and traceable
operation, unlike the legacy placeholder endpoint.

**NUT-005** A meal plan contains ordered meal slots. Each slot may offer multiple recipe
choices. The system distinguishes:

- target macros/calories for the day;
- macros for each individual choice;
- total for a concrete set of selected choices;
- an optional statistical average across alternatives.

"Average macros per meal" cannot be used as the client's actual consumed total unless a
specific choice was recorded.

**NUT-006** Assigning a diet from a library creates a client-specific versioned snapshot
linked to the subscription, following the same template rule as training. Client logs and
notes refer to the snapshot/choice that existed on that date.

**NUT-007** `plan.docx` uses **BMR** incorrectly for the 2,500-calorie value after applying
activity/training intensity and as the basis for weight-loss calories. BMR is resting energy
need. The calculated maintenance estimate is **TDEE**, and the coach-selected number (for
example 2,000) is the **calorie target**. These must be separate fields:

```text
BMR estimate -> activity model -> TDEE estimate -> coach goal adjustment -> calorie target
```

Training intensity alone is insufficient for TDEE; activity/steps and the chosen formula
must be explicit. The selected BMR/TDEE formula, coefficients, inputs, units, and version are
stored in a calculation snapshot.

**NUT-008** Given a validated calorie target, the requested simplified macro calculation is:

```text
proteinCalories = proteinGrams * 4
fatCalories = calorieTarget * fatPercentage
fatGrams = fatCalories / 9
carbohydrateCalories = calorieTarget - proteinCalories - fatCalories
carbohydrateGrams = carbohydrateCalories / 4
```

The command is rejected when percentage is outside its allowed range, grams are negative,
or protein plus fat exceeds the target. Rounding happens only for display/prescription after
the unrounded values are retained. If fiber, alcohol, or label-specific energy is supported,
the simple 4/4/9 policy must be extended explicitly.

**NUT-009** If the coach enters no calorie target, calculations use the current approved TDEE
estimate. Entering a target creates a new calculation snapshot; old diet assignments retain
the snapshot they were based on.

**NUT-010** Allergy and medication data may warn the coach but the app must not claim medical
diagnosis or guarantee recipe safety. Ingredient/allergen completeness and clinical scope
require legal and domain review before release.

## 7. Bodyweight and progress

**PRG-001** A client can have at most one daily bodyweight observation per tenant and local
calendar date. It records the actual measurement date, value/unit, author, timestamps, and
correction history.

**PRG-002** "Daily null by default" is represented by no observation row, not a fake row with
zero/null weight. The UI renders every date in the current period and shows missing dates as
empty.

**PRG-003** On entry, the backend resolves which active subscription/program contains that
local date and links the observation appropriately. A payload cannot attach a weight to
another client's program or an out-of-period date.

**PRG-004** Week summaries use the program's date mapping. Mean bodyweight uses only valid
observations and shows the number of observed days; missing days are never zero. A week with
no measurements has no mean.

**PRG-005** End-of-mesocycle change compares explicitly defined statistics (proposed: first
complete/available weekly mean versus last complete/available weekly mean), not arbitrary
single days. The exact comparison rule needs coach approval and is stored with the summary
version.

**PRG-006** Training, diet, subscription, and progress views synchronize through shared
identifiers and authoritative periods, not copied date fields. A period change triggers a
validated rescheduling workflow and identifies any logs that would become out of range.

## 8. Messaging, notifications, media, and libraries

**MSG-001** A conversation belongs to a tenant and an explicit participant set. A tenant
membership alone does not grant access to every conversation. Messages are persisted before
delivery and carry server timestamps, sender, edit/delete status, and delivery metadata.

**NOT-001** In-app, email, and WhatsApp notifications share a domain notification but maintain
separate delivery attempts. Retries are idempotent; provider acknowledgement is not confused
with user read status.

**MED-001** Object bytes live in object storage; the database owns metadata, tenant, purpose,
content type, size, checksum, status, and retention. Upload authorization validates type and
size, uses generated object keys, and includes malware/content scanning before publication.

**MED-002** Exercise media is associated with a versioned exercise and can be assigned into a
program snapshot. Default clients see only assigned/permitted media. A coach may grant a
client explicit full-library access per tenant.

**MED-003** Coaches may receive download controls while clients receive short-lived streaming
URLs, but a web application cannot guarantee that viewable media is impossible to copy.
Product language must not promise absolute download prevention.

**LIB-001** Recipe and exercise/video libraries are separate tenant-owned catalogs. Public or
provider-sourced records, if later added, are copied/referenced under explicit licensing and
cannot leak one coach's private library into another tenant.

## 9. Theme and gamification

**GAM-001** A tenant can choose a standard theme, uploaded assets, or generated assets. Media
still follows validation, rights, moderation, and storage rules. Theme changes do not rename
backend concepts or API contracts; localized display labels are presentation configuration.

**GAM-002** Experience is an append-only ledger driven by authoritative workout/diet events.
Rank and level are derived from the ledger and a versioned rule set. Replayed events do not
award twice. Corrections create compensating entries.

**GAM-003** Losing experience for missed workouts needs an exact definition of scheduled,
excused, rescheduled, partially completed, and late activity. It should not be implemented
until product and coaching impact are approved.

**GAM-004** The requested Solo Leveling theme uses third-party intellectual property. Do not
ship names, artwork, characters, or trade dress without documented rights. A generic
coach-configurable progression theme is the safe default.

## 10. Planned database protections

At minimum, later migrations should enforce:

| Invariant | Database protection |
| --- | --- |
| One membership per tenant/user | Unique `(TenantId, UserId)` |
| One client identity link per tenant | Filtered unique `(TenantId, UserId)` |
| One tenant client email | Unique normalized `(TenantId, Email)` per approved policy |
| No overlapping live subscriptions | Partial GiST exclusion on tenant, client, date range |
| Valid service period | Check `EndExclusive > Start` |
| One daily bodyweight | Unique `(TenantId, ClientId, LocalDate)` |
| Positive bodyweight/height/load | Check constraints with approved limits |
| One completion per assigned day | Unique `(TenantId, AssignmentDayId, ClientId)` |
| Valid macro inputs | Non-negative checks; aggregate validation in domain transaction |
| Tenant-safe child relationship | Composite FK including `TenantId` where practical |
| Concurrency | PostgreSQL `xmin` token plus conflict handling |

Database constraints complement domain validation. Friendly validation happens before save,
and constraint violations are translated into stable API problem responses.

## 11. Approved Phase 1 decisions

Approved on 2026-08-20 and recorded under `docs/adr/`:

1. A tenant is a logical coaching workspace. A solo coach owns one directly; no physical gym
   or parent company is required. The same model supports multi-coach businesses later.
2. One global account may participate in multiple independent workspaces. Every client
   profile and every coaching fact remains isolated per workspace.
3. Coach owners self-register and receive an owned workspace. Clients are invite-only in
   Phase 1. Email confirmation is required.
4. Client-editable and coach-only fields follow ADR 0004. Account email changes require a
   separate verification flow.
5. Lebanon launches first with English source text, localization-ready UI, configurable
   workspace currency, and initial `USD`/`Asia/Beirut`/Monday/`en-LB` defaults.
6. Phase 1 is adults-only. Email delivery is implemented first; WhatsApp remains behind a
   future provider integration.
7. Platform support has no implicit tenant-data access. Any future support access is
   explicit, time-limited, and audited.

## 12. Decisions required before later phases

1. Does one subscription always bind training and diet to one period, or can each service
   have independent dates? Can an active subscription have no training program?
2. Are installments supported? Exactly which payment standings grant full access, and does
   an overdue client retain historical content?
3. What is the behavior when a program starts midweek or a coach changes dates after logs
   exist?
4. Which BMR formula, activity/TDEE model, unit conventions, and sex/formula options are
   approved? Who is responsible for clinical review?
5. Is macro energy strictly 4/4/9, provider calories, or both with discrepancy reporting?
6. Which RPE/RIR reference table and progression model is the first supported version? What
   plate increments and rounding policy apply?
7. Can clients truly edit accessory prescriptions, or only log actuals/request a change?
8. What counts as diet completion and a missed workout for experience changes?
9. Do you own or license all uploaded/imported recipes, videos, generated art, and any themed
   intellectual property?
