it follows, and section 3 collects the choices the rows made on their own, for the owner. The decision page and the
plan it came from are private (the plan holds names and prices); the rows carry everything a chat needs.

## 1. How a row is built

**Where.** On this laptop, by the plan runner: `.\scripts\start-plan-runner.ps1 -Plan notes`, one fresh chat per Open
row on Opus 5.5 at max effort, with ultracode on: the chat orchestrates its understanding of the area, the build and an
adversarial review of its own diff with the Workflow tool (the owner's word of 5 November). A chat builds the first Open
row in section 0's order, commits on main once its checks are green, and the runner pushes each row that ends Done.
Nobody watches: the owner reads the reports and the screenshots afterwards.

**What every row keeps.**

- The rules of AGENTS.md: the backend is the source of truth and an invariant never lives only in Angular; every
  tenant-owned row carries its tenant and its tests include the unauthorized and wrong-tenant cases; history that
  AGENTS.md names append-only stays append-only; no money rule, discount figure, price or legal wording is invented
  by a chat. docs/UI-REDESIGN-PLAN.md gives the kit, the words and the definition of done for every screen.
- The words. A coach is never called "verified", "certified", "licensed", "qualified" or "approved" anywhere on a
  screen, in a notification or in a document the app writes. The badge's words are "Founding coach". A certificate
  line names what was shown and when; its wording is one constant, because the lawyer may reword it.
- Nothing sensitive (health answers, documents, identity data) reaches logs, analytics, exceptions or notification
  payloads. Certificate records hold text only: never a copy, a photo or a screenshot of a document.
- The first download has a 520 kB error budget (510 kB warning): a web row measures the production build's initial
  size before and after and names both numbers in its report.
- Where a row changes a rule, it updates DOMAIN-RULES.md, the ADR or ARCHITECTURE.md in a few lines, as AGENTS.md
  asks. No new docs, reports or checklists.

**Checks**, in proportion to what changed (AGENTS.md). A row that changes server code builds the backend and runs the
full suite with `scripts/TestPostgres.ps1` and a container name of its own, about 1 h 30 on this laptop, before it
commits; a failure an earlier row left is fixed first, in its own commit, and named in the report. A web row runs
`format:check`, `lint`, the production build, Vitest and `git diff --check`, plus the mocked Playwright specs of the
screens it changed, not the whole suite. Every new or changed screen gets a live pass against a fresh demo in a
throwaway database of the chat's own (`ConnectionStrings__Database` pointed at it, built with the `demo-workspace`
command), never against the owner's API (port 5134), web (port 4200) or the `tbgym` and `tbgym_demo` databases: 390 px
and 1440 px stills in light and dark into `artifacts/notes-plan/<row>/` (git-ignored), axe clean, no sideways scroll,
200% text still readable. The chat drops only the database it created and stops only the servers it started.

**Choices.** A technical question is the chat's (AGENTS.md). A product choice the row and the docs do not settle (the
words, a layout, a default, an edge case) is built the way the row's "Open" part names, or the way closest to the
owner's earlier choices, and listed in section 3, "Waiting for the owner" (the row, the question, what was built, the
other way, a recommendation). A new money rule, legal wording or work outside the row is left out and listed there
with a recommendation; the rest of the row is built. No chat stops the run for a decision. A row too big to finish well
in one chat is split into parts (N4a, N4b), each with its scope: the first is built and committed, the others stay
Open for the next chat.

**Commits.** A chat stages only the files its row changed, by explicit path, commits once its checks are green, then
records the hash in its status row in a second small commit. No co-author or "Generated with" lines. It never pushes;
the runner does, once the row ends Done.

**Builds.** Everything is in the web app; the server does not change. The rule: Plausible counts only the website's own pages, and only for a visitor who is not signed in. The pages are two, the ones that load the script today: the homepage (`/`) and What changed (`/changelog`) when nobody is signed in; one count each time one of them is opened. ### N3a · Founding status, the server

**Follows**

- Decision 15 (5 Nov 2026): a founding coach earns the badge once at least 10 of their clients have an active plan and they have stayed past the trial month and at least one more month; kept after the discount ends; removed only for cause. Whether the wait is 2 or 3 months was left to the build: both thresholds are published terms the admin changes in a click, 10 clients and 2 months at first, and the row says so in section 3.
- The plan's Point 8, founding coaches from your list: the words are "Founding coach", never "verified", "certified" or "qualified"; cause means misrepresented qualifications, harm to clients, or the coach asks; the platform admin marks a space as founding, with the date, and removes the badge with a reason, history kept; certificate lines (decision 7) are a separate build, so a coach whose certificate nobody can confirm can still be a founding coach; the badge changes no bill.
- Decision 10 (5 Nov 2026) fixed the founding places, so the two docs that still say "first 10" are aligned in this row.
- What exists today: the admin gives a space a percentage off with dates (`WorkspaceDiscount`: granted, revoked once, kept) on the space's page under Platform billing; nothing marks a space as founding; the only platform-wide settings are the price plan's immutable versions, whose first version the ManualPlatformBilling migration seeds with an INSERT.

**Builds**

Server only: no screen changes in this row. Every screen is N3b, built on this row's API, the way B8a built Energy v2's server and B8b its screens.

1. Founding status. One row per mark in the platform billing module, `billing.FoundingStatuses` (`FoundingStatus : TenantEntity`): `FoundingSince` (a UTC day, like every billing date), `Note` (what it is for, required, 200 characters, as the discount's), `MarkedAtUtc`, `MarkedByUserId`; `EarnedOn`, `EarnedUnderTermsVersion`, `EarnedClientCount` (written once, never cleared); `BadgeHidden` (the coach's own switch, drawn in N3b); `EndedAtUtc`, `EndedByUserId`, `EndReason` (required, 500 characters). One open status per space (a partial unique index on `TenantId` where `EndedAtUtc` is null); a space may be marked again after an ended one, so the rows are the history. A database trigger refuses deleting a row, changing its mark, clearing or changing the earned fields once written, and touching an ended row, the way `protect_workspace_discount` guards discounts.
2. The terms. `billing.FoundingTerms` (`FoundingTerms`), global, immutable, versioned like `PlatformPricePlan`: `VersionNumber`, `ClientsWithActivePlan` (1 to 1,000), `MonthsSinceStart` (0 to 24), `Note`, `PublishedAtUtc`, `PublishedByUserId`, append-only by trigger; the latest version is in force. Version 1 is 10 clients and 2 months, an INSERT in this row's migration, the way the first price plan is seeded (the ManualPlatformBilling migration, lines 471-479). Neither number is a constant in code. N8 adds the price guarantee's months to the same table later, as a new version.
3. The rule, named and versioned: `FoundingBadgePolicy` v1 in the module. On a UTC day a space's badge is NotFounding (no open status, or `FoundingSince` after today), Waiting (clients so far of clients needed; whole months since the space's creation day of months needed), Earned (its day) or Ended (its day and reason). Months are whole calendar months from `Tenant.CreatedAtUtc`, as the trial counts from it (31 January plus one month is the last day of February). Clients with an active plan are counted the way Billing counts clients with a plan (`PlatformBillingReader.CountAsync` with `BillableClientCounter`: paid or free coverage, a pack's too, unpaused), but over a window of this instant rather than the month so far, so a plan that ended earlier in the month does not count; the Billing page's month-so-far figure can read higher, and the admin's panel (N3b) says it counts right now. Once Earned, the state stays Earned whatever the counts do later; only an end for cause changes it.
4. The check. `FoundingBadgeCheck` in Infrastructure: for every open status not yet earned, count, evaluate, and write `EarnedOn` (today), the terms version and the count, once; safe to repeat. `PlatformBillingWorker.RunOnceAsync` runs it after the invoice run and the reminders on every sweep (every 15 minutes by `SweepIntervalSeconds`; there is no daily schedule, and a repeat finds its work done); the admin runs it on demand.
5. API, the admin's (the existing `/api/platform-admin` group: the platform-admin policy, antiforgery, the sensitive-write rate limit):
   - `POST /workspaces/{tenantId}/founding` `{ foundingSince, note }` → the status; 404 for an unknown space, 400 for a day after today or an empty note, 409 `founding_exists`.
   - `POST /founding/{foundingId}/end` `{ reason }` → the status; 404, 400 for an empty reason, 409 `founding_ended`.
   - `POST /founding/check` → `{ checked, earned }`.
   - `GET /founding-terms` → every version; `POST /founding-terms` `{ clientsWithActivePlan, monthsSinceStart, note, expectedCurrentVersion }` → 409 `founding_terms_changed`, like `price_plan_changed`.

**Rules**

- Only the platform admin reads or writes the record: the `PlatformAdmin` policy re-reads the account and role on every request; an owner, a coach or a client of any space answers 403, anonymous 401, and no workspace header is read. No route takes a coach's or a client's id from a workspace member.
- A line can be added only for a user who is active staff (Owner or Coach) of that space at that moment; otherwise 404, as a missing coaching space answers on Platform billing.
- Tenant scope: the row is tenant-owned, so the global query filter hides it from any read inside another space and the write-scope guard refuses a write outside the space the admin set; the admin's list reads every space with `IgnoreQueryFilters`, and N4b's reads inside a workspace see their own space's lines alone.
- Append-only where it matters, in the database: once confirmed, `What`, `ShownOn`, `CoachUserId`, `TenantId`, `RecordedByUserId`, `ConfirmedAtUtc` and `ConfirmedByName` never change and the row is never deleted (a trigger with error code 55000, like `TR_LegalDocumentVersions_Protect`); the one later change allowed to those columns is the erasure, which blanks the text once together with `ErasedAtUtc`, and nothing changes after that. A waiting line may be deleted: nothing about a "not confirmed" result is kept. Checks: `What` and `ConfirmedByName` are empty and `ShownOn` is null exactly when `ErasedAtUtc` is set; `ConfirmedByName` is set exactly when `ConfirmedAtUtc` is; `TakenDownAtUtc` only on a confirmed line; `RequestedOn` only with reason `Request`; `ErasedByUserId` set exactly when the reason is `Request`; one not-erased line per coach, space, words and day (a unique index with that filter).
- Confirm, drop, take down and erase carry the `version` the admin saw; an older one answers 409 `certificate_line_changed`; an action that does not fit the line's state answers 409 `certificate_line_state`.
- The server refuses, as whole words in `What`, the four reserved words ("Verified", "Licensed", "Qualified", "Approved": the platform's promises, not a coach's proof) and the plan's protected health titles ("physiotherapist", "physical therapist", "physio"), which Point 2 allows only with an Order check that decision 7 does not make. The refusal is 400 on the field and never repeats the word: "This line holds a word TB Gym keeps out of a coach's line. Write what the proof is: the certificate, the gym, the years."
- No copy, screenshot or file is accepted anywhere: no upload, no media asset, no link field. The record is text only.
- Nothing of a line reaches a log, an exception, a notification or analytics (CLI-008): a refusal names the field, never the value; the erase answer and any log line say counts only.
- No line, or none, changes anything about a coach's account, trial, bill, clients or sign-up: access and money never read this table. It is a sentence and nothing more: no ranking, no points, no badge.

**Rules**

- The guarantee holds a plan version, never a price typed by hand; every number still comes from a published version (PBL-003 amended to say which one).
- Invoices stay immutable snapshots; the version chosen is written on the invoice as today, so every bill is explainable.
- No FX, credit, refund or recurring billing; nothing changes for a space that is not founding; nothing changes between a coach and their client.
- A revoked discount is out of the count: the last unrevoked discount's last day starts the N months. The plan's way to end a founding price early is the admin's two steps, revoke and grant again to the month's end, and the guarantee then counts from that end. An ended founding status ends the guarantee from the next invoice (see Open).

## 3. Waiting for the owner

Choices the rows made on their own, each with the other way. Nothing here blocks a row.

- **N1, the sign-up page's count.** Decision 23 (still to explain) says what the counts include; until it is picked, N1 counts the homepage and What changed for a visitor alone, and the sign-up page's one count is left for N2's chat. The other way: count it in N1 now. Recommendation: wait; it is one line either way.
- **N1, where the two counted addresses live.** The homepage (`/`) and What changed (`/changelog`) are one constant, `COUNTED_PAGES` in `core/website/website-analytics.ts`, which the browser's guard reads; the homepage counts itself at `/` alone, so a second address for it (N2's `/about`) counts nothing. The other way: list them beside the routes. Recommendation: keep; a website page added to the count is one word there.
- **N1, a section link on the homepage.** Following The week, What's inside or For gyms (an address ending in `#…`) counts nothing more, since the visitor stays on the one page. The other way: a count per section, which Plausible's `hashBasedRouting` would give. Recommendation: keep it off; the sections are one page.
- **N1, What changed for someone signed in.** It reads no website settings at all, so nothing of analytics loads there, and after a full page load it decides only once the session check has answered. The other way: read the settings and only never count. Recommendation: keep; the page inside the app needs nothing from them.
- **N1, Plausible's other events.** A new site at Plausible tracks outbound links, file downloads and forms sent by default; the browser turns those three off and its guard drops any event but a page view of the two pages, so a tap on the WhatsApp link or a Talk-to-us form sent is never counted, whatever is ticked at Plausible, and none can fire from a page of the app. The other way: let those switches work on the two pages. Recommendation: keep until you want a form sent counted; it is then one line, like decision 23's sign-up count.
- **N1, a tab opened behind and a return from the browser's cache.** As Plausible's own counting did, a page opened in a background tab or prepared ahead by the browser counts when it is first shown (not at all if it is closed unseen), and a page the browser brings back from its back/forward cache (Back after leaving the site in the same tab) counts again. The other way: count such a return nothing, since it runs no app code; the catch of counting it is that someone who signed in from another tab meanwhile is counted as the visitor this tab still shows. Recommendation: keep; it is what Plausible counts on any other site.
- **N1, what a count carries.** A count sends the page's path and the campaign tags Plausible reads (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`, `ref`, `source`) and no other part of its address, fragment included, so a link tagged `?utm_source=instagram` still shows where visitors came from while nothing else in an address reaches Plausible. The other way: send the whole address, as Plausible's own script does. Recommendation: keep.
- **N1, outside the row: the Talk-to-us form before the page is live.** On a slow connection a gym can send the form before the homepage's code has started; the browser then sends it the old way and puts the name, gym, contact and message in the homepage's address (`/?name=…`), which stays in the browser's history and in nginx's log. N1 keeps it out of the count; the form itself is not changed here. Recommendation: the sign-in page's remedy (Send waits until the page is live, the fields carry no `name`, and `check-prerender.mjs` pins both) as a small row of its own.
- **N1, outside the row: What changed's Skip to content.** Its link (`href="#changelog-content"`, no handler) resolves against the page's base address, so pressing it loads the homepage instead of moving to the releases, and that visit is counted; the app's shell and the legal pages use a `skipToContent` handler for exactly this. Not changed here. Recommendation: the same handler, a one-line fix with a test.
- **N3a, the badge's thresholds.** Decision 15 fixed "at least 10 clients with an active plan" and "after the trial month and at least one more month" but not whether the wait is two or three months, so both thresholds are platform settings with the defaults 10 and 2, changeable by the platform admin without a build. The other way: 3 months. Recommendation: keep 2 until the two of you say 3.
- **N4a, the certificate line's wording.** The line reads "<what was shown>, shown to TB Gym on <date>", one constant in the code, because the lawyer may reword it (Point 2's step 1). The other way: wait for the lawyer before building. Recommendation: build now; the words change in one place.
- **N4b, the keeping period.** The lawyer sets how long a certificate record is kept; until then the setting holds the plan's example (while the account exists, plus 12 months after the last line is removed). The other way: no default, nothing kept until the lawyer answers. Recommendation: the example, since a record without a keeping period would be the worse breach.
