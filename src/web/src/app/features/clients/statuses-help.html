Clients and Today follow the same rules. A reason counts only while the client’s plan includes what it is about, so a plan that has ended shows none of its check-ins or workouts.

What needs you

Each tag on a client’s row opens where you deal with it. Today lists the same reasons under Needs you, in this order.

Check-ins to review
They sent a check-in you haven’t reviewed. It clears once you review it.
Unread messages
They wrote to you and you haven’t read it yet. Only your own chats count. It clears once you read them.
Asked to renew
Their plan has ended and they asked to renew it in the last 30 days. It clears once a new plan is booked, paid or not, or after 30 days.
Joined, no plan yet
They joined in the last 14 days and have no plan at all. It clears once you start one.
Week not shared
A week of their program has started, or starts within 2 days, and isn’t shared with them yet. It clears once you share it.
Missed workouts
Two or more workouts were due and not started since the last day they started one, counting back up to 14 days. It clears once they start a workout, a missed one done late included.
Check-ins overdue
A check-in was due 2 to 13 days ago and they haven’t sent it; one they started but didn’t send still counts. It clears once they send it, or on the 14th day after it was due. Remind opens your chat with them when you coach them and messaging is in their plan.
No program yet
Training is in their plan, but no program is running or coming. It clears once you assign one.

Their plan

The plan’s own status sits beside the reasons. A plan ending soon is on Today’s list too, but it doesn’t count as needing attention.

The plan is running.
Its last paid day is within 14 days, today included: 13 days away is soon, 14 is not. A renewal that is paid moves that day; one waiting for payment doesn’t.
It was paused. It still ends on its last day.
Nothing covers today: the plan ended, hasn’t started or is waiting for payment, or there is no plan yet.
Their training, progress and check-ins stay closed to you and to them until they are unblocked on Service & access. Their plan and its dates don’t change.
What is left on their session packs, paid and not over, while a pack runs. It turns this colour and joins Needs you at 2 sessions or fewer, or when the last pack’s last day is within 14 days, today included. A pack paid for and still to come counts; one waiting for payment doesn’t. It doesn’t count as needing attention.
