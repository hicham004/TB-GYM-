Money received
Payments on the day the money arrived, part payments too, before any costs. Each currency stays on its own line.
1-on-1 sessions held
Sessions held, on their own day, for whoever held them. A charged no-show and a session undone don’t count.
Coaching plans and session packs sold
A coaching plan is the coaching a client pays for over dates; a session pack is a set of 1-on-1 sessions. Each counts on the day it was booked, renewals too, paid yet or not. A free one doesn’t count, and neither does one cancelled before any money came in. A single session is a pack of 1.
Clients started
Their first day on a plan or pack, or their first after a gap. One still waiting for payment doesn’t count yet.
Clients ended
The day their last plan or pack ended or was cancelled, with nothing right after it. A renewal that follows on isn’t an end.
Whose numbers
Everything but a session counts for the coach the client had that day, so a client who moves on leaves their earlier numbers behind.
Last month
It can still change: a payment or session recorded late counts on its own day.
