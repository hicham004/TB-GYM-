# What changed

Every change a coach or a client can see, newest first. A heading is the day it shipped
(`## YYYY-MM-DD`); each line under it is one change in the words of the people who use it. The
homepage prints the newest date, and `/changelog` prints the whole file, at build time.

## 2026-10-13

- The homepage, rebuilt: one week of coaching told from both sides on real screens of a sample
  coaching space, and this page.

## 2026-10-12

- Share cards: a client turns a new personal record or a finished program into an image in their
  coach's colours, made on the phone, and shares or saves it. TB Gym posts nothing.
- Before and after: a client picks two of their own progress photos of one pose and makes a
  before-and-after image, after a confirmation that names the photos and their dates.
- The program builder is denser: the exercise library opens as a rail beside the week, each
  exercise is one line, rest days are thin strips, and a week folds to a one-line summary.

## 2026-10-11

- The workout player starts every set from what the coach prescribed, shows last time beside it,
  says when rest is over, and fills the screen without the tab bar.
- A client's Today, Training and Check-ins follow their light or dark choice.
- A page that cannot be opened says so in a toast with Retry; a client who is not yours says so
  instead of showing an empty page.
- Every sheet asks before an unsaved change is lost, whichever way the page is left.
- Suggested training maxes round to plate steps; the strength history reloads after the maxes
  are updated.

## 2026-10-10

- The coach's two check-in pages, the coach's workout page and the Training player's empty and
  status states are on the new kit, and the last of the older styling is gone.
- The coach's client Nutrition tab: targets worked out in a sheet from the client's record, meal
  plans assigned after an overlap check, allergens read and saved in a sheet.
- The coach's Strength history: every max by lift with how it moved, Record a max, and how the
  client has done, in sheets.
- Weigh-ins, photos and measurements on one Your entries page; removing a photo frees its date
  and pose.
- My profile and the intake: five short steps for a client, their answers as cards with an Edit
  sheet, and the coach's view of the same.
- Invitations: who is waiting to join first; Invite a client, Send again and Cancel one press away.
- Settings, Team and Billing on one Settings layout; the platform admin's screens rebuilt.
- Notifications: training notices for clients, the inbox in days with All and Unread, settings in
  cards.
- The exercise library as cards with their media, filter chips kept in the address, one sheet to
  add or edit.
- Plans you sell: each plan a card with its price options and the clients on each.
- One max history per lift: the client's Training max history lists every program's training
  maxes beside the recorded ones.

## 2026-10-08

- A suggested max can be kept; maxes to review appear on Coach Today; a next program starts where
  the last one ended.
- The Training maxes panel suggests a max from the client's best logged set.
- Recipes for clients: a plan keeps its own copy of each recipe, and the client opens it in a sheet.

- Meal plans: plan cards and the meal-plan builder with the coach's draft.
- Progression: the next block with the builder's steps, Edit weeks, exercise demos and notes.

## 2026-10-06

- Training maxes belong to a program and update the coming weeks' weights after a preview; a
  program has a name.
- Progression: Duplicate with progression in the builder, and loads worked out from the RPE chart.
